Site name

The site is called Embraer Aircraft Gallery.

Embraer is a Brazilian aerospace company that produces commercial, military, executive, and agricultural aircraft.

Gallery was added because aircraft are presented one model at a time, each with a photo and some basic information, similar to how exhibits are displayed in a "real" gallery.

Purpose

The Embraer Aircraft Gallery is dedicated to showcasing some significant aircraft produced by Embraer, providing enthusiasts and general visitors with a comprehensive resource for historical aviation artifacts and information.

Period covered by the site is 1969 to present.
